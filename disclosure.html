How we test paddles

Our recommendations are built on independent, repeatable criteria rather than manufacturer claims:

  • Swing weight — how the paddle feels in motion at the net.
  • Twist weight — a measure of forgiveness on off-center hits.
  • Grit retention — how long the face holds spin after hard play.
  • Core type & thickness — foam vs. honeycomb, 14mm vs. 16mm.
  • On-court feel — drives, dinks, resets, hand battles.

When we cite a specific paddle, it reflects testing or closely analyzed measured data — not a sponsored placement. No brand pays to appear on a "best" list.
