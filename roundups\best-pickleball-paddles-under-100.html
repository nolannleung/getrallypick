Best pickleball paddles under $100

Updated July 2026 · 3 picks, plus sale-watch notes

Cheap paddles used to mean wood and regret. Not anymore. A few carbon-faced paddles now sell under $100, and one of them is good enough to keep past your first year. The catch is that truly sub-$100 paddles are rare, and most "budget" lists inflate the count by counting sale prices as the real price. I've kept this honest.

Of the eight paddles I tested, only the Vatic Pro Prism Flash holds under $100 at full price. The Selkirk Amped S2 regularly drops to around $100 on sale from a $159.99 list. Everything else I tested starts at $149.99 or more. I've flagged each price clearly so you know what you're actually looking at.

Buying tips that actually save money

  • Watch the live price. Our links pull the current Amazon number, and paddles move $20 to $40 during holidays. The MSRP is a starting point, not the price you'll pay.
  • Buy last year's colorway. Brands discount old colors hard, and the paddle is the same.
  • Skip the $30 specials. The surface grit wears out in weeks and the core dies fast. The Vatic at $99.99 is the floor I'd trust.
