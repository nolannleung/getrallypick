Onix Evoke Pro — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Power · $199 MSRP

The Evoke Pro is Onix's lively, pop-forward paddle, and the composite face gives it a springy response you feel on every hit. It is a natural fit for tennis converts who already swing hard and want that familiar pace. At $199 it is one of the cheaper power options here, which makes the punch easy to forgive.

What stood out

  • Springy pop. The composite face launches drives with less effort than raw carbon.
  • Familiar feel. Tennis players will recognize the pace and swing naturally.
  • Low price. At $199 it delivers real power without a premium bill.

What to weigh

  • Less raw spin. The composite face grips less than a gritty carbon build.
  • Touch takes care. The pop can run your soft game long if you're not careful.

Who it's for

Tennis players moving into pickleball, or anyone who wants easy pace on a budget, will like the Evoke Pro. Players chasing kitchen touch will want a softer, more controlled face.

Verdict

Best budget power A lively, affordable power paddle that feels natural to hard swingers, especially from a tennis background.
