Engage Encore Pro MX 6.0 — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Control & Spin · $149.99 MSRP

The Encore line is the reason "spin" became a selling point in pickleball, and the MX 6.0 keeps that going. Its FiberTEK face is a chemically treated fiberglass that grabs the ball, so dinks dip and resets sit soft. The "MX" is Engage's elongated shape at 16.5 inches, which gives more reach than the standard Encore while keeping the same control-first feel.

What stood out

  • Spin you can feel. The treated face bites the ball. Dinks dip, resets sit soft.
  • Big sweet spot. The elongated face still forgives off-center contact well.
  • Net touch. Soft and quiet at the kitchen. Placement players will love the feedback.

What to weigh

  • Fiberglass, not carbon. Less raw pop. You generate pace rather than borrow it.
  • Heavier feel up top. The elongated body swings a touch slower than a thin paddle.

Who it's for

If your game is built on placement, spin and patience rather than blasted drives, the Encore Pro is a natural fit. New to shot selection? Start with our how-to-play guide first.

Best spin & control The classic spin paddle, still the standard for touch players who win with placement.
