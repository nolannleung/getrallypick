Engage Encore Pro MX 6.0 — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Control & Spin · $139.99 MSRP

The Encore Pro is the paddle that made "spin" a household word in pickleball. Its chemically treated fiberglass face grabs the ball and lets you shape dinks and resets with uncanny feel. It's the control player's control paddle — quiet, soft, and built to win points with placement rather than pace.

Specs at a glance

CorePolymer Honeycomb (MX thick core)
FaceChemically Treated Fiberglass (Control Pro)
ShapeWidebody (MX = Max)
Weight7.8–8.3 oz
Best forControl, spin, all-court

What we liked

  • Spin you can feel. The treated face bites the ball — dinks dip, resets sit soft.
  • Huge sweet spot. The widebody face forgives off-center contact better than most elongated builds.
  • Net touch. Soft and quiet at the kitchen; placement players will love the feedback.

What to consider

  • Fiberglass, not carbon. Less raw "pop" — you generate pace rather than borrow it.
  • Heavier feel up top. The widebody swings a touch slower than a thin elongated paddle.

Who it's for

If your game is built on placement, spin and patience — not blast drives — the Encore Pro is a natural fit. New to shot selection? Start with our how-to-play guide first.

Verdict

Best spin & control The classic spin paddle, still the standard for touch players who win with placement.
