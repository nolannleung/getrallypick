CRBN TruFoam Genesis 4 — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Forgiving · $279 MSRP

The TruFoam Genesis 4 is CRBN's bet that a foam-filled core beats a hollow one for players who win points with placement. The face sits soft against the ball and holds it longer than almost anything I've tested, which makes dinks and drops feel almost automatic. If you'd rather outthink the court than outhit it, this one is built for you.

What stood out

  • Deep dwell time. The foam core grabs the ball so you can shape your shot instead of just blocking it.
  • No dead spots. Off-center hits stayed live, which kept my resets honest late in long rallies.
  • Soft, confident touch. Dinks landed where I aimed instead of skating off the face.

What to weigh

  • Not a power paddle. You'll manufacture pace with your swing. Flat bangers will want more pop.
  • Premium price. At $279 it asks more than many control paddles that play close to it.

Who it's for

If your game is built on the kitchen line, drops, and patient resets, the Genesis 4 rewards that patience. Newer players who still mishit often will like the forgiveness, but the price only makes sense once you trust your swing.

Verdict

Best for control A control paddle that turns dwell time into real shot-making, best for players who win with placement.
