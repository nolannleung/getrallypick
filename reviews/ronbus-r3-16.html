Ronbus R3.16 — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Forgiving · $149 MSRP

The Ronbus R3.16 is a consistent, predictable paddle that rewards players working on placement. The raw carbon face spins, and the 16mm core keeps the ball steady through the kitchen. I found my third shots landing in the same spot point after point.

What stood out

  • Predictable response. Every hit felt the same, which built trust in my placement.
  • Spinny raw face. The textured carbon bit hard on topspin and cuts.
  • Forgiving 16mm core. Off-center hits stayed calm and in play.

What to weigh

  • Soft power. Put-away drives need a committed swing to finish.
  • Less reach. The hybrid shape is quick but not built for extra length.

Who it's for

Placement players building a consistent game will feel at home. It suits control-focused intermediates and rec competitors.
