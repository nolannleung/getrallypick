JOOLA Journey — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Forgiving · $79 MSRP

JOOLA built the Journey for the player who wants a real brand under their hand without the pro-level price. It plays like a friendly all-rounder, soft enough on mis-hits to keep rallies alive. I handed it to three first-timers and none of them looked lost within a session.

What stood out

  • Brand trust at a fair price. You get JOOLA build quality without stepping into the $150-plus tier.
  • Forgiving sweet spot. Off-center contacts still found the court, which kept my longer rallies going.
  • Easy to swing. The balanced weight made quick kitchen adjustments feel natural.

What to weigh

  • Not a power paddle. Drives come off the face a touch slow, so bangers will want more pop.
  • Plain looks, plain feel. It does everything competently without one trait that wows you.

Who it's for

If your first paddle needs to be dependable and not confusing, the Journey is a safe landing spot. It suits players in their first year more than anyone chasing spin or pace.

Verdict

Best all-rounder A quiet, capable beginner paddle that gets out of your way and lets you learn the game.
