Holbrook Fuze — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Power · $200 MSRP

Holbrook's Fuze is built to pop. A stiff, thin-core build with a long handle makes it a favorite for players who want pace on tap and a swing that reminds them of tennis. At $200 it is a value play for the aggressive crowd.

What stood out

  • Easy pop. The face returns pace fast, so your drives and serves carry weight.
  • Tennis-friendly handle. The long grip suits a two-hander and adds reach.
  • Light on the wallet. You get real power without the tour-price tag.

What to weigh

  • Soft game suffers. That same pop makes dinks skittish until you soften your hand.
  • Not for finesse. Control-first players will find it loud and unforgiving.

Who it's for

For the banger and the tennis convert who wants pop and a long grip at a fair price. Not a first paddle for the patient placer.

Verdict

Power A budget-minded power paddle with a lively face and a long handle.
