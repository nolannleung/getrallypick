Selkirk LUXX Control Air Epic — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Control · $199.99 MSRP

The LUXX Control Air asks a fair question: what if a paddle quietly taught you to play the right way? It trades raw power for a face that rewards placement, and for newer players that trade is the habit worth building first. Selkirk recently updated it to the InfiniGrit surface and slimmed the core from 20mm to 19mm, which adds a touch of pace without losing the plush control feel.

What stood out

  • Placement first. The soft, tacky face lets you shape dinks and drops instead of spraying them.
  • Stable through the swing. The Epic shape keeps mishits centered and predictable.
  • Confidence builder. Testers reported cleaner third-shot drops within a single session.

What to weigh

  • Limited pop. You won't overpower anyone. You build points the smart way.
  • Not for bangers. If you live at the baseline swinging hard, a power build will feel better.

Who it's for

If you're in your first year and want a paddle that nudges you toward smart placement, the LUXX is a near-perfect teacher. Pair it with our guide to choosing a first paddle and you'll skip a lot of bad habits.

Verdict

Best control A teaching paddle that pays you back in cleaner shots. Buy it if you want to learn the game the right way.
