Gamma Needle — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Forgiving · $59 MSRP

Gamma's Needle is the lightest kind of starter, an elongated paddle that barely registers in your hand. It lets a new player focus on form instead of wrestling equipment. I liked how little fatigue I felt after a long drill block.

What stood out

  • Featherlight. The low weight meant no wrist strain late in a session.
  • Extra reach. The elongated shape covered balls I would otherwise miss.
  • Easy to swing. Quick hands at the kitchen came without effort.

What to weigh

  • Light means less plow-through. Heavy balls can push the face around.
  • Thin grip. Players with larger hands may want an overgrip.

Who it's for

Good for players who want to build form without heavy gear getting in the way. It suits juniors and anyone coming back from a wrist issue.

Verdict

Ultralight starter A near-weightless paddle that keeps the focus on your technique, not the equipment.
