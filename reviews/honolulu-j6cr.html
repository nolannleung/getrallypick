Honolulu J6CR — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Power · $175.50 MSRP

The J6CR is Honolulu's value bomb. It is the most explosive paddle under $200 we have seen, made for the player who wants to hit hard from day one without overspending. The trade is touch, which you learn to manage.

What stood out

  • Big bang, low price. Few paddles under $200 pop this hard off the face.
  • Ready power. Drives and serves jump, so you can pressure from the baseline early.
  • Simple to swing. The build feels light and fast in the hand.

What to weigh

  • Control is the cost. The pop that helps your drive can float your dinks.
  • Not forgiving. Off-center hits lose pace and the soft game needs a soft hand.

Who it's for

For the budget-minded player who wants power now. If you are still building touch, give it a few weeks before judging.

Verdict

Best value power The cheapest real power paddle here, and it earns the label.
