Rally Tyro 2 Pro — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Forgiving · $79 MSRP

The Tyro 2 Pro is Rally's step up from the rock-bottom models, and the better materials show. It plays balanced with a touch more pop than the cheapest paddles, good for someone ready to take the game a bit seriously. I noticed cleaner feedback on clean hits.

What stood out

  • Clear upgrade. Better face and core than the ultra-cheap tier.
  • Balanced feel. Enough pop to learn drives, enough touch for dinks.
  • Honest price. A sensible middle rung before premium paddles.

What to weigh

  • Still a stepping stone. You will likely outgrow it as you improve.
  • Average spin. The face is decent but not a spin specialist.

Who it's for

Good for players moving past their first paddle who want a real improvement without a big jump in cost. It bridges the gap better than most at this price.

Verdict

Best transitional A clear step up from ultra-budget gear, and a smart transitional paddle.
