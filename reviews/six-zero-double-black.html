Six Zero Double Black — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Power · $189 MSRP

The Six Zero Double Black is the brand's attack-minded build, a step up in aggression from the Black Opal. The 16mm foam core still cushions, but the raw face and elongated shape push hard on every swing. I found myself stepping into balls I normally blocked.

What stood out

  • Attacking pop. The foam core launches drives with a punch that quieter paddles lack.
  • Raw-carbon grip. The 3D face held spin through fast, short exchanges at the net.
  • Long reach. The elongated shape extended my court coverage on wide balls.

What to weigh

  • Demands commitment. It is built to attack, so passive players will fight the sweet spot.
  • Firmer than the Opal. The faster face trades some plushness for pace.

Who it's for

Players who want to dictate points and come forward will love this. It is less suited to those still building a soft game.

Verdict

Best for aggressive players A forward-leaning power paddle for players who like to attack.
