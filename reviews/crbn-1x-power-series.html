CRBN-1X Power Series — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Power · $179.99 MSRP

The 1X is CRBN's answer to one question: how do you add pace without giving up the spin a raw-carbon face is known for? It leans into drives and put-aways while keeping enough touch for the kitchen. If the all-court benchmarks feel a little tame, this is the paddle that turns up the volume.

Specs at a glance

Core16mm Polymer Honeycomb (13mm also offered)
FaceRaw T700 Carbon Fiber
ShapeElongated (1X)
Weight7.8–8.2 oz
Best forIntermediate to advanced, power seekers

What we liked

  • Real drive pop. Off the baseline it launches drives that all-court paddles only hint at.
  • Raw-carbon spin. The grit holds through long sessions, so shape on your third shot stays reliable.
  • Extra reach. The elongated shape buys you a step at the net without feeling unwieldy.

What to consider

  • Less forgiving than a widebody. Mishits sit up more — this rewards cleaner mechanics.
  • Premium over value builds. You're paying for the power tune; newer players may not feel it yet.

Who it's for

If you've got the basics down and want pace you can trust, the 1X is the cleanest step up. Still deciding between control and power? Our paddle matcher narrows it in five questions.

Verdict

Best for driving power A raw-carbon paddle that finally prioritizes pace — without going numb at the net.
