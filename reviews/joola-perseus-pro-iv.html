JOOLA Ben Johns Perseus Pro IV (16mm) — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · All-court · $299 MSRP

The Perseus Pro IV is the paddle that keeps topping independent "best overall" lists, and after testing it, the reason is simple: it's the rare paddle that doesn't have a real weakness. Power, control and spin all sit at a high level, which is exactly why it works for advancing players who've outgrown a beginner paddle but aren't ready to specialize.

Specs at a glance

Core16mm Propulsion Honeycomb
FaceTextured carbon fiber (CFS)
ShapeWidebody (largest sweet spot in line)
Weight7.8–8.2 oz
Best forIntermediate → advanced, all-court

What we liked

  • Balanced everywhere. Drives, resets and dinks all feel natural — no "dead" zone.
  • Forgiving edges. Foam-injected edge guard expands the sweet spot to the paddle's corners.
  • Fast to learn. No awkward adjustment period; it feels playable from the first rally.

What to consider

  • Price. At ~$299 it's a commitment — fine as a "keep for years" paddle, steep as a first one.
  • Not a beginner starter. New players won't feel the difference vs. a $120 paddle yet.

Who it's for

If you've been playing 6+ months, want one paddle that carries you from intermediate to serious, and value all-court flexibility over a single extreme, the Perseus Pro IV is our top recommendation. If you're brand new, start with a $100 paddle and upgrade later.

Verdict

Best overall A genuine do-it-all paddle. Buy it once, grow into it, and it won't hold you back as your game improves.
