Six Zero Black Opal — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · All-court · $250 MSRP

The Black Opal is Six Zero's all-court statement: a foam-core build that's explosive from the baseline and quick at the net, with grit that actually lasts.

Specs at a glance

Core16mm Foam (Cold Fusion) Core
FaceRaw Carbon Fiber, Long-Life Grit
ShapeHybrid
Weight7.8–8.2 oz
Best forIntermediate → advanced, all-court

What we liked

  • Baseline pop. The foam core returns energy on drives you can feel.
  • Net speed. The compact shape moves fast for hands battles.
  • Grit that holds. 25+ hours of spin without going slick.

What to consider

  • Firm feel. Less plush than a thick polymer core; takes a session to adjust.
  • Price. $250 is real money for a smaller name, but the build justifies it.

Who it's for

If you've moved past beginner and want one paddle for both ends of the court, the Black Opal is a top pick. Compare it against our top overall if you want the deepest all-court option.

Verdict

Best all-court (foam) A do-almost-everything build with a modern foam core.
