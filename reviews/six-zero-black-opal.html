Six Zero Black Opal — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · All-court · $249.99 MSRP

The Black Opal is Six Zero's all-court statement. It runs a thin 14mm foam core with a Power Gel layer, so it's explosive from the baseline and still quick at the net, and the Diamond Tough face keeps its grit far longer than raw carbon usually does. The shape is a hybrid, not a widebody, which surprised a few testers who expected something softer.

What stood out

  • Baseline pop. The foam core returns energy on drives you can feel.
  • Net speed. The compact shape moves fast for hands battles.
  • Grit that holds. The Diamond Tough surface stays bitey well past the point where raw carbon goes slick.

What to weigh

  • Firm feel. Less plush than a thick polymer core. It takes a session to adjust.
  • Price. $250 is real money for a smaller name, but the build justifies it.

Who it's for

If you've moved past beginner and want one paddle for both ends of the court, the Black Opal is a top pick. Compare it against our top overall if you want the deepest all-court option.

Verdict
