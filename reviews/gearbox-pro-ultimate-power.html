Gearbox Pro Ultimate Power — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Power · $333 MSRP

The Pro Ultimate Power is Gearbox at full volume. Its thin SST carbon core is the loudest, most explosive build I tested this year, and it punishes any ball you catch clean. Just know it demands clean mechanics, because there is little forgiveness in a paddle this tuned for pace.

What stood out

  • Explosive pace. Clean drives leave the court before your opponent reads them.
  • Surprising stability. The solid carbon core keeps the head steady through fast swings.
  • Decisive finishes. Put-aways at the net feel final, not hopeful.

What to weigh

  • Unforgiving. Mishits drop dead. You earn every bit of that power with technique.
  • Steep price. At $333 it is the costliest paddle here, and the value only shows up in a strong hand.

Who it's for

This is for advanced players who already finish points and want a paddle that adds pace without asking questions. If you still hunt for the sweet spot, spend less and grow into it.

Verdict

Best for power A specialist power build that rewards skilled, aggressive hands and quietly punishes everyone else.
