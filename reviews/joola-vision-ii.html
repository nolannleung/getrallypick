JOOLA Vision II — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · All-court · $179 MSRP

The JOOLA Vision II is the brand's middle-ground option, and it splits power and control about as evenly as I have seen. The hybrid shape keeps the swing quick while the face still drives. It is not the most forgiving stick, so it fits players already past the basics.

What stood out

  • Even power-to-control split. I could drive and drop with the same paddle without resetting my game.
  • Quick hybrid shape. The 16.2-inch frame moved fast in hands battles at the net.
  • Familiar JOOLA feel. If you have played the pro line, this reads close at a friendlier cost.

What to weigh

  • Not forgiving. Mishits drop off fast, so weak technique gets exposed.
  • Middle of the price band. At $179 it costs more than the value all-rounders without topping the class.

Who it's for

Improving intermediates who want one paddle for both ends of the court will get the most here. It rewards players already building consistency.

Verdict

Best all-court (mid) A balanced all-court paddle for players past the beginner stage.
