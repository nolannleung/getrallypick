Selkirk Neo — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Forgiving · $89 MSRP

The Neo is Selkirk's way of putting its name on a paddle that costs less than a dinner out. It leans control, the thing Selkirk does best, and it stays soft when you need it most. I found my dinks landing flatter than usual, which is the exact habit newer players struggle with.

What stood out

  • Control you can feel. The face slows the ball just enough to shape soft shots.
  • Selkirk forgiveness. Mishits stayed in play far more than I expected at this price.
  • Comfortable grip. The handle felt sized right for smaller hands and long sessions.

What to weigh

  • Low pace. You won't win rallies by hitting through people with this one.
  • Simple build. It skips the premium surfaces found on Selkirk's upper lines.

Who it's for

New players who like the idea of learning control early will get along with the Neo. It rewards patience over power, which is a good first lesson.

Verdict

Best control A patient, forgiving teacher that costs less than most Selkirk paddles and plays like a bigger one.
