Bread & Butter Fat Boy Widebody — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Forgiving · $189.99 MSRP

The Fat Boy earns its name: a widebody face so forgiving it's almost impossible to mishit, built to smooth out your mechanics while you learn.

Specs at a glance

Core16mm Polymer Honeycomb
FaceCarbon Fiber
ShapeWidebody (largest sweet spot)
Weight7.7–8.1 oz
Best forBeginner, forgiveness

What we liked

  • Huge sweet spot. Off-center hits still go where you aimed.
  • Calm and stable. Great for players who tense up under pressure.
  • Easy to trust. You stop worrying about the paddle and watch the ball.

What to consider

  • Less reach. A widebody means a shorter overall length than elongated shapes.
  • Not a power paddle. Placement and consistency are the whole point here.

Who it's for

If you're new and tired of framing balls into the net, the Fat Boy is therapy. Pair it with our first-paddle guide before you buy.

Verdict

Most forgiving The gentlest way to build real consistency.
