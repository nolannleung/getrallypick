Uteeq Carbon Edge — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Power · $89 MSRP

Uteeqe's Carbon Edge is one of the few carbon paddles under $100 that actually pops. It is built for players who want pace without paying premium prices, and it delivers more drive than most budget paddles. I enjoyed how easily balls sailed off the face.

What stood out

  • Real pop. The carbon face drives the ball harder than its price implies.
  • Carbon at a deal. Raw carbon under $100 is rare and worth noting.
  • Good reach. The elongated shape extends your court coverage.

What to weigh

  • Less touch. The pop makes soft dinks a bit trickier to learn.
  • Forgiving? Not really. Mishits sting more than on control paddles.

Who it's for

Perfect for power-hungry beginners who want pace and do not mind trading some control. If you like ending points early, this is your budget pick.

Verdict

Best budget power A rare sub-$100 carbon paddle with genuine pop, built for players who want pace.
