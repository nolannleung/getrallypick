Paddletek Element — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Forgiving · $69 MSRP

Paddletek's Element is built light and control-minded, which makes it a calm teacher. The soft polymer core rewards placement and lets you feel the ball instead of fighting it. I found it easiest to use when I slowed down and aimed.

What stood out

  • Light in the hand. The low weight kept my wrist happy through long sessions.
  • Touch-focused. Dinks and drops felt natural, like the paddle wanted to help.
  • Forgiving face. Off-center contact stayed manageable.

What to weigh

  • Not much pop. Drives need a full swing to clear the net with pace.
  • Smallish sweet spot for its class. You feel mishits more than on a widebody.

Who it's for

Ideal if you want to learn proper technique and value touch over power. It is the paddle I suggest to players who keep getting told to slow down.

Verdict

Best for control A light, control-first paddle that teaches good habits from the first session.
