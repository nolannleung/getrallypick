CRBN³ Core Entry — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Forgiving · $99 MSRP

CRBN made its name with premium carbon paddles, and the Core Entry brings that name to the sub-$100 shelf. It keeps the raw carbon face the brand is known for, with a forgiving shape that suits newer players. I felt the family resemblance to their pricier models right away.

What stood out

  • True CRBN feel. The raw carbon face has the bite the brand is known for.
  • Forgiving shape. The elongated body still helps off-center hits.
  • Trusted name. You get brand build quality without the premium price.

What to weigh

  • Premium-ish price. It is the costliest on this budget list.
  • Firm feedback. The carbon face is crisp, which some new players find less soft.

Who it's for

The pick if you want a recognized carbon brand and real quality while staying under $100. It suits a beginner who plans to stick with the sport.

Verdict

Best brand quality CRBN's entry paddle brings genuine brand quality to the budget segment.
