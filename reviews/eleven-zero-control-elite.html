Eleven Zero Control Elite — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Forgiving · $219 MSRP

The Control Elite is Eleven Zero's gentle teacher. It is built wide and soft, so off-center hits still find the court and you can focus on where the ball goes instead of whether you caught it clean. At $219 it is priced for the player who cares more about feel than firepower.

What stood out

  • Forgiving face. Mishits stay in play, which lets you swing free while you learn placement.
  • Soft response. The thick core eats pace, so dinks and drops settle instead of popping up.
  • Confidence boost. Testers stopped second-guessing their contact and just played.

What to weigh

  • Low ceiling. You won't out-hit a banger. Points are built, not blasted.
  • Too calm for some. If you like a lively face, this one will feel dead at first.

Who it's for

For the improving player who wants a paddle that covers mistakes while they sharpen placement. Good from roughly the 2.5 level up to the low 4s.

Verdict

Forgiving A forgiving control paddle that builds skill before it demands it.
