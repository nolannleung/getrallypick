Gearbox CH1 — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Power · $169 MSRP

The Gearbox CH1 is a different animal, built on the brand's Solid Span core that feels almost like one solid piece. It pushes the ball hard off the face and the elongated handle suits anyone crossing over from tennis. My drives surprised partners who expected a softer Gearbox.

What stood out

  • Real baseline pop. The Solid Span core returns energy you can feel on full swings.
  • Long handle. The 5.5-inch grip let my second hand sit comfortably for two-handers.
  • Plush-ish for a power paddle. The carbon face still reads contact better than I expected.

What to weigh

  • Unforgiving on mishits. Off-center hits punish you, so it demands clean technique.
  • Heavier swing. The elongated build is not the quickest at the net in fast hands battles.

Who it's for

Power players and tennis converts who want a long handle and a firm, fast ball will like this. It is less kind to players still fixing their swing.

Verdict

Best for power + tennis converts A firm, powerful paddle for players who already hit clean.
