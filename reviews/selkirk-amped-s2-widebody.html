Selkirk Amped S2 Widebody — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Beginner · $100 MSRP

The Amped S2 is the lightest paddle on our list and the easiest to swing — a widebody built for older beginners, smaller players, and anyone with wrist concerns.

Specs at a glance

Core16mm X5 Honeycomb
FaceFiberFlex Fiberglass
ShapeWidebody (S2, shortest handle)
Weight7.3–8.2 oz
Best forBeginner, lightest swing

What we liked

  • Whippy and light. Wrist-friendly and great at the kitchen.
  • Big face, calm feel. Fiberglass softens contact and builds confidence.
  • Real Selkirk build at $100. Trusted name without the premium price.

What to consider

  • Fiberglass, not carbon. Less raw grit than carbon faces, but easier to control.
  • Short handle. Two-handers may want more length to work with.

Who it's for

If a heavy paddle wears out your arm, the Amped S2 is the fix. Start with our how-to-choose guide to confirm the fit.

Verdict

Lightest / best beginner The lowest-swing-weight way to start clean.
