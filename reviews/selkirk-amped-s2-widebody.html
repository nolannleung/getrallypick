Selkirk Amped S2 Widebody — Review

Reviewed July 2026 · Beginner · $159.99 MSRP

The Amped S2 is one of the easiest paddles to swing in this group, and the widebody face makes contact forgiving. It's built for older beginners, smaller players, and anyone whose wrist complains about a heavy paddle. The current AMPED Control version uses a fiberglass face tuned for control and a 16mm X5 core, and it goes on sale often enough that the real-world price usually sits closer to $100.

What stood out

  • Whippy and light. Wrist-friendly and great at the kitchen.
  • Big face, calm feel. Fiberglass softens contact and builds confidence.
  • Real Selkirk build at a fair price. Trusted name without the premium tag.

What to weigh

  • Fiberglass, not carbon. Less raw grit than carbon faces, but easier to control.
  • Short handle. Two-handers may want more length to work with.
