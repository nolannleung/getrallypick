Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026

Who we are

getrallypick is an independent pickleball paddle guide and review site. We help players in the United States choose gear through original articles, hands-on testing notes, and a free paddle matcher. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run and improve the site.

  • Information you provide. We do not currently ask for personal information through sign-up or checkout. If you contact us (for example, to flag an outdated spec or request a correction), we use the information you send only to reply.
  • Information collected automatically. Like almost every website, our hosting provider and analytics tools may log standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device, referring page, and the pages you view. This data is aggregated and used for security and to understand overall traffic — not to identify you personally.

Cookies and similar technologies

We and some of our partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve relevant ads, measure performance, and remember basic preferences.

  • Google AdSense (our advertising partner) may use the DoubleClick cookie to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google at Google Ad Settings, and from many third-party ad networks via the Network Advertising Initiative and YourAdChoices.
  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Disabling cookies may affect how some features display.

Advertising

This site displays third-party advertising through Google AdSense. These vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing. Advertising is kept clearly separate from our editorial content and never influences our reviews, rankings, or paddle matcher results.

Affiliate links (Amazon Associates)

Some links on getrallypick point to Amazon (US) as part of the Amazon Associates program. If you click one and make a purchase, Amazon may set cookies and collect purchase-related data under its own privacy policy. getrallypick does not receive your personal details from Amazon. Our commissions never change what we recommend.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand aggregate traffic (for example, which guides are most helpful). These tools may set cookies that you can disable in your browser.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction. If you have shared information with us, you can ask to see it or have it corrected or deleted by contacting us.
  • United States (California / CCPA). We do not sell your personal information. California residents may request access to or deletion of personal information we hold.
  • European Economic Area (GDPR). You have rights to access, correct, and object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise them.
  • Children. getrallypick is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach us through the contact details on our Contact page.
