Best pickleball paddles for power

Updated July 2026 · 4 picks from hands-on testing

Power comes from a few things working together: a thinner core, a firm face, and an elongated shape that gives you leverage on the swing. If you want to out-drive opponents from the baseline, these are the paddles I'd reach for. I tested eight. Three are clear power builds, and one budget option surprised me.

Our picks

A note on core thickness

Core thickness is the easiest power dial on a paddle. The LUXX at 19mm is the softest and most controlled paddle I tested; the Black Opal at 14mm is the liveliest. Most paddles sit at 16mm as a compromise. If power is your only goal, go thin and elongated. If you want pace you can still control at the net, the 16mm all-court builds are the safer bet.
