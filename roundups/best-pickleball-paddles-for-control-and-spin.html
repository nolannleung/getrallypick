Best pickleball paddles for control & spin

Updated July 2026 · 5 picks from hands-on testing

Control and spin reward patience. A thick core slows the ball, a textured face grabs it, and an edge-free design keeps the response even across the face. If your game lives at the net with dinks, drops, and topspin rollbacks, these are the paddles I'd pick. I tested eight; the five below are the ones that actually let you place the ball where you want it.

Our picks

A note on face texture

Spin comes from surface grit more than anything else. InfiniGrit (LUXX) and Diamond Tough (Black Opal) are the two longest-lasting textures I tested. FiberTEK (Engage) grabs hard but the treated fiberglass wears faster than raw carbon. Expect to re-evaluate grit after a season of regular play, and rotate paddles if you play several times a week.
