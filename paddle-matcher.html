💡 Paddle buying basics (read first)

Three specs decide how a paddle feels and plays:

  • Weight (7.5–8.2 oz): lighter = more control, heavier = more power. Most players start midweight.
  • Core thickness (14–16mm): thicker = softer touch & more forgiveness; thinner = more power & pop.
  • Shape: widebody = biggest sweet spot (easiest to learn); elongated = more reach & power.

Our matcher turns these into a shortlist for your game — then read the full reviews before you buy.
